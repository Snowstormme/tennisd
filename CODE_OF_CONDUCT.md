# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Tennisd is a community for people who care about tennis, software and useful discussion. We are committed to making participation welcoming, respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or point of view.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- welcome questions, different opinions and good-faith disagreement;
- focus criticism on ideas and code, not people;
- protect other people's privacy and avoid sharing personal data;
- follow the repository license and respect project, API and content terms.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, hate speech, deliberate disruption, spam and publishing someone else's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to the repository, Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, project chats and other spaces where someone represents Tennisd. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content, restrict participation, or close a thread when necessary to protect the community. Decisions are made in the interest of a safe and constructive project.

## Reporting

Please report a problem privately to [notsnowstorm@proton.me](mailto:notsnowstorm@proton.me). Do not post sensitive details publicly. Include a link or location, a short description of what happened, and any relevant evidence with personal data redacted.

Reports will be handled confidentially where possible. We aim to acknowledge reports within 7 days.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
