YOUR DATA

Privacy.

Tennisd is a tennis diary and community archive. This page describes how this installation handles account data.

What we store

We store your username, email address, a one-way password hash, profile name, bio and optional profile photo, match diary entries, ratings, reviews, comments, followed players and tournaments, and watchlist. If you send product feedback or answer a poll, we store that response and may associate it with your account when you are signed in. Guest poll votes use a random browser identifier stored in the session and saved only as a one-way hash. Uploaded profile photos are resized and converted to WebP before storage. Short-lived verification and password reset tokens are stored as hashes. We keep hashed request-limit records for up to one day to slow abuse.

What others can see

Your username, display name, bio and public diary entries can be seen by visitors. Comments on public reviews are public. Private diary entries and your watchlist are visible only to you. Your email address and password hash are never shown on public pages.

Why we use it

We use this information to run your account, show the tennis diary and community discussions, understand product problems and priorities, send account verification and password reset emails, and protect the service from abuse. We do not sell account data or show ads.

Where it goes

The website runs on Render, account data is stored in a PostgreSQL database, and account emails are sent through a transactional email provider. These providers may process technical request logs under their own policies. The site uses a session cookie to keep you signed in.

Your controls

You can export your account data, make diary entries private, delete individual entries and comments, or delete your account from Settings. Active database records are removed when you delete your account. If encrypted backups are enabled, older copies may remain for up to 30 days before expiry.
