WHY TENNISD EXISTS

A home for
tennis memories.

Matches are more than results. Tennisd is a place to remember the ones you watched, share a review, and follow the players who keep you coming back.

THE DIARY

What you can do

Browse ATP and WTA matches, rate a match from 1 to 10, write a review, mark favorites, keep entries private, comment on public reviews, and follow players. Your profile turns those entries into a personal view of the tours and surfaces you watch.

THE DATA

Honest numbers

Current and upcoming matches come from Live Tennis API. When a singles match finishes, Tennisd keeps the same match permanently with its final score and winner. Older results may still belong to the discontinued Jeff Sackmann catalog while its removal audit protects existing diary entries; each result page states its source and date precision.

Player win rates, titles and head-to-head records are calculated only from matches loaded into this site's catalog. They are not claimed as full career records. Current rankings are not provided; where shown, a rank is the one recorded at that player's latest loaded match.

PLAYER PHOTOS

Wikimedia portraits

Where a player has a Wikidata portrait, Tennisd displays the corresponding image from Wikimedia Commons. Each file remains governed by the license and attribution on its Commons description page. Players without a linked portrait receive a Tennisd placeholder.

PRIZE MONEY

Source linked

When a player has a Wikidata ID, Tennisd may show its reported career prize money in USD with its source and date. Wikidata is community edited and may be out of date. If there is no verified USD amount, the site shows a dash instead of an invented figure.

LICENSE & ATTRIBUTION

Non-commercial use

Live Tennis API supplies the current match feed. The Odds API can supply clearly labelled Pinnacle market estimates. Any remaining legacy Jeff Sackmann rows retain their CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 attribution until they are removed.
